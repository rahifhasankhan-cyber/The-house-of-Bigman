THE HOUSE OF BIGMAN — WEBSITE PACKAGE
====================================

CONTENTS
- index.html: homepage
- services.html: central directory of all six service divisions
- weddings.html: BigMan Weddings
- commercial.html: BigMan Commercial
- hospitality.html: BigMan Hospitality
- property.html: BigMan Property
- studios.html: BigMan Studios
- content.html: BigMan Content
- pricing.html: guide prices and monthly retainer packages
- portfolio.html: selected-work layout and case-study publishing framework
- team.html: parent brand, flexible crew model and workflow
- contact.html: project enquiry page
- style.css: shared design and responsive styling
- script.js: responsive navigation, form validation, and mailto hand-off

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract all files into the same folder.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. Keep all HTML files, style.css and script.js together.

IMPORTANT BEFORE LAUNCH
1. Confirm the enquiry recipient email in script.js. It currently uses hello@thehouseofbigman.com.au as an unverified placeholder. The form opens a visitor's email app; it does not submit to a server or store enquiries.
2. Replace all stock/placeholder images with owned or properly licensed production images, including real behind-the-scenes photos.
3. Add at least three genuine case studies. Each should cover the client's objective, the creative approach, deliverables and only approved/verified results.
4. Add real client testimonials only after receiving permission. Do not publish invented reviews or performance claims.
5. Review the indicative AUD pricing on pricing.html against your own production costs, supplier quotes and local market. Confirm whether GST is included or additional before publishing prices as final.
6. Add confirmed Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, domain and other official account links when they exist. No social account URLs have been invented in this bundle.
7. Validate the business name, domain, trade mark position, business structure, permissions, insurance and contract templates with the relevant Australian sources/advisers before trading.
8. Confirm any required privacy information and the final way enquiries will be processed before going live.

SOURCE-BASED NOTES
- The six divisions and pricing/retainer figures are based on the supplied business plan.
- Price ranges are labelled indicative because the source document says they are planning ranges, not verified market tariffs.
- Placeholder imagery is for visual layout only and must not be presented as completed client work.
