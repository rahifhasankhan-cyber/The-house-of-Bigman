03 HOW THE HOUSE WORKS

One lead.
The right crew.

The model is intentionally lean: one parent brand and one coordinated workflow, with trusted freelance specialists brought in when the brief requires them.

CREATIVE LEAD

Direction & client relationship

The founder leads creative direction, client communication, core production and final quality control.

FLEXIBLE CREW

Specialists when needed

Depending on the brief, the production may include camera operators, lighting, sound, photo or video editors, colourists, motion designers, stylists, makeup artists, talent or production coordination.

PROTECTED DELIVERY

Care at every hand-off

Projects include a written scope, agreed payment milestones, organised file handling, backup checks, client review and a final delivery check.

STANDARD PROJECT WORKFLOW

Enquiry → discovery call → brief and budget → proposal and contract → deposit → pre-production → shoot → backup and ingest → editing → client review → final delivery → balance payment → testimonial or retainer discussion.
